# Acme Corp — Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Overview

Acme Corp provides full-time employees with 20 days of paid time off (PTO)
per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month.
Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis matching their
scheduled hours.

## Requesting Time Off

Employees must submit PTO requests through the HR portal at least 5
business days in advance for requests of 3 days or fewer, and at least 15
business days in advance for requests longer than 3 days. Manager approval
is required for all PTO requests.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the following calendar
year. Any unused PTO beyond that cap is forfeited on December 31st, except
where prohibited by local law.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and is not subject to the
carryover cap. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year.

## Parental Leave

Acme Corp offers 16 weeks of paid parental leave for the primary caregiver
and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver, in addition to standard PTO.
