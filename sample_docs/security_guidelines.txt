Acme Corp — Information Security Guidelines

1. Password Requirements
All employee accounts must use passwords of at least 14 characters,
combining upper and lower case letters, numbers, and symbols. Passwords
must be rotated every 180 days. Reuse of the previous 5 passwords is
prohibited.

2. Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for all systems that handle customer data, source code,
or financial records. Acceptable MFA methods include hardware security
keys and authenticator apps. SMS-based MFA is discouraged but permitted
as a fallback.

3. Device Policy
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and automatic
screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices used to
access company email must be enrolled in the mobile device management
(MDM) system.

4. Data Classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
Restricted data (e.g. customer PII, payment information) may only be
stored in approved, encrypted systems and must never be emailed
unencrypted.

5. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident — phishing email, lost device, unusual
account activity — must be reported to security@acmecorp.example within
1 hour of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate independently.

6. Third-Party Access
Vendors requiring access to internal systems must go through a security
review and sign a data processing agreement before credentials are
issued. Vendor access is reviewed quarterly and revoked immediately upon
contract termination.
